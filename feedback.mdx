Feature requests and votes shape what we build next. You can reach the feedback board, the roadmap, and the changelog from the web, and the first two are also built into your dashboard.

## Request features and vote

The public feedback board runs on Featurebase. Submit ideas, describe your use case, and vote on existing requests. The most-voted items get attention first.

## From your dashboard

You do not have to leave the app. The dashboard sidebar has **Feedback** and **Roadmap** entries under Resources that open the same board and roadmap inside your account.

## Changelog

Track releases, improvements, and fixes on the [Changelog](/changelog) tab in these docs. That is the single source of truth for what changed and when.

## Bugs and support

For a bug or a technical issue, email [support@memoryplugin.com](mailto:support@memoryplugin.com) rather than posting on the board. Direct support is faster for anything broken.
