Gemini is Google's assistant at [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com). MemoryPlugin gives it long-term memory that carries across your other AI tools, through the browser extension.

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Install the [browser extension](/integrations/browser-extension) and sign in to your MemoryPlugin account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Gemini">
    Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com). A floating MemoryPlugin button appears on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate for the chat">
    Click the button to inject memories from your selected bucket into the composer, then send your message. This also tells Gemini how to save new memories as you talk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The MemoryPlugin floating button on gemini.google.com. Click it to inject memories or open the panel.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/platforms/gemini.png" alt="MemoryPlugin floating button on Gemini (gemini.google.com)" />
</Frame>
## What works here

- **Memory recall and save.** Injected memories give Gemini context; new memories are saved back as you talk.
- **Extract memory pill.** Select text in a chat and click **Extract memory** to save that snippet.

## Limitations

- **Chat history sync is experimental.** Gemini sync works through the extension, but Gemini's interface changes without warning, so this can break. If you hit an issue, contact support through our website.
