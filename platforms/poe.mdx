Poe is Quora's app for chatting with many AI models in one place, at [poe.com](https://poe.com). MemoryPlugin adds long-term memory that carries across your other AI tools, through the browser extension.

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Install the [browser extension](/integrations/browser-extension) and sign in to your MemoryPlugin account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Poe">
    Go to [poe.com](https://poe.com). A floating MemoryPlugin button appears on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate for the chat">
    Click the button to inject memories from your selected bucket into the composer, then send your message. This also tells the model how to save new memories as you talk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The MemoryPlugin floating button on poe.com. Click it to inject memories or open the panel.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/platforms/poe.png" alt="MemoryPlugin floating button on Poe (poe.com)" />
</Frame>
## What works here

- **Memory recall and save.** Injected memories give the model context; new memories are saved back as you talk.
- **Extract memory pill.** Select text in a chat and click **Extract memory** to save that snippet.

## Limitations

- No chat history sync here. Direct sync covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, and TypingMind only.
