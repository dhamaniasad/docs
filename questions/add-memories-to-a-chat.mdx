Click the MemoryPlugin button in the chat composer (or the **Add Memories** pill above the floating button) before you send your first message. That one click pastes your memories and the saving instructions into the chat.

<Warning>
The extension does not inject memories on its own by default. Until you activate a chat, the AI cannot read your memories or save new ones in that conversation.
</Warning>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a bucket">
    Open the panel with the floating MemoryPlugin button and choose a bucket from the dropdown (it defaults to **All Memories**). The AI gets the memories from whichever bucket is selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the chat">
    Click the quick inject button inline in the composer (tooltip: *"Inject Memories from chosen Bucket"*). On platforms where the inline button doesn't fit, click the glass **Add Memories** pill above the floating button instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your message">
    Your memories plus instructions for saving new ones are now part of the conversation. From here the AI knows you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What actually gets added

Activation pastes two things into the composer:

1. Your memories from the chosen bucket.
2. Instructions telling the AI what MemoryPlugin is and how to save new memories.

## Skip the manual click

Turn on **Automatically add memories in new conversations** in the extension's Settings tab. See [How do I add memories to new chats automatically?](/questions/auto-inject-memories)
