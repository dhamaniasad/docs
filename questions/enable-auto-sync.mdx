Open the extension panel, go to the **Settings** tab, and flip on the platforms you want under **Background Sync**. From then on the extension syncs new conversations from each enabled platform once an hour, with no file exports involved.

<video controls playsInline className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/enable-auto-sync.mp4"></video>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the floating MemoryPlugin button on a supported site, then open the **Settings** tab in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Background Sync per platform">
    Toggle on the platforms you want: **Auto-sync ChatGPT**, **Claude**, **Gemini**, **Grok**, **DeepSeek**, or **TypingMind** conversations. Each platform has its own switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check on it any time">
    Use **View Sync Status & History** in the same section, or open the **Sync** tab to see what is synced and what isn't.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Auto-sync needs you signed in to both MemoryPlugin and the source platform in that browser. Gemini sync is experimental and may break when Google changes things.
</Note>

## Syncing manually instead

If you'd rather sync on your own schedule, open the **Sync** tab and use the **Sync** dropdown: **Sync All**, **Sync current page**, or **Choose chats...**. Progress shows as *"{x} of {y} unsynced chats"* and you can cancel mid-run.

## Have a big backlog?

For hundreds of old conversations, uploading an export file from the dashboard is faster than syncing chat by chat. See [Importing Chat History](/features/chat-history/importing).
